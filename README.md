# Slimic

English | [Korean](README.kr.md)

> Raise slimes and put them to work as your companions.

Slimic is a Minecraft Forge mod about raising friendly slimes and directing them as assistants.

## Overview

Slime companions grow through shared stats and activity-based upgrades. They can be named, managed, and carried as items while retaining their traits. Mining slimes excavate terrain, transport slimes move cargo and supply tools, and herald slimes signal stalled work. Dye colors distinguish companions, while a Creative Slime Tuner supports custom stats. JEI provides crafting guidance.

Slimic is in early development within the `0.1.x` series.

## Platform

Minecraft **1.20.1**, Minecraft Forge **47.x**, and Java **17**.
